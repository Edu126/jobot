# REQ-048: The coach acknowledges like a person and lets you finish

Date: 2026-10-08
Status: Built 2026-10-08 ([ADR-074](../decisions/ADR-074-acks-restate-a-detail-and-2-5s-pause.md))
Source: Eduardo, testing live voice practice on -edu (sessions 44 and 45). He spoke this feedback into the session.

## What they asked for
> "Mm-hmm", "uh-huh" sounds weird, the way it acknowledges my answers doesn't feel natural. It should be like "okay, I hear you, thank you for your comments", or reply something related, in a short way. If it is something positive the person is saying, acknowledge what they're saying.

> You got to give me a little bit of like 5 seconds-ish just to confirm that I'm done, because if not I'm still building my idea.

## What they actually need
- **To feel heard.** A bare "mm-hmm" after a 30-second answer sounds like the coach wasn't listening. A short reference to what was said shows it was.
- **Room to think.** Real candidates pause mid-answer. A coach that jumps in after 0.7 s trains you to rush, which is the opposite of what practice is for.
- The coach still doesn't praise or judge during the interview (REQ-044 register, P7 rules).

## How we'll know it worked
- Eduardo runs a session and doesn't notice the acknowledgments. Nothing sounds off.
- He can pause to think mid-answer without the coach taking the turn.
