# REQ-055: Landing page with a recorded product film

Date: 2026-10-12
Source: Eduardo (website and login question pages, 2026-10-12)
Status: Open

## What they asked for
"It should look made by an agency." A short product film in the style of
Apple and Wealthsimple: "big zoom in on job search, animations, zoom out,
tailor typing, demo practice." The film is recorded, not a live practice
(cost and abuse). The headline is "The support team behind your job
search." The feeling is trust. English only. The single action is
joining the beta. Proof: honest numbers, a privacy promise, real quotes. A
short founder section.

## What they actually need
Strangers on Reddit have to trust a new tool with their résumé within 10
seconds. The film shows the real product (fit score, voice coach, gap map)
instead of describing it. It avoids every AI-site cliché: glow, robots,
buzzwords, stock photos, fake logos and walls of text.

## How we'll know it worked
Out of the visitors who reach the page from the post, at least 1 in 10 join the waitlist.
