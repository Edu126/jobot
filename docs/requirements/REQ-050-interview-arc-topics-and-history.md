# REQ-050: An interview arc, a topics checklist, and past feedback always reachable

Date: 2026-10-10
Source: Eduardo, after -edu session 48
Status: Building

## What they asked for
"Questions gotta follow an arc, not a random question and then another random
question… start with tell me about your career, then something personal, then
drift to something more professional." At setup: "is this two, three, five
competencies? One should be personal and behavioral… more like checklist
style, so the person can know how many things are gonna be evaluated… the more
they ask, the bigger the chances to get one of each." And: "a place where the
person can see the previous results — the feedback tab always; if you go there
you see the records and may open previous ones; if you were practising you go
to the one you just did." Also: the on-screen aids during the interview "are
awful, not conversational, not easy to read" (open — next).

## What they actually need
A rehearsal that flows like a real interview, a setup that says exactly what
will be evaluated, and a history of every rehearsal to see progress.

## How we'll know it worked
Setup shows a checklist and a question count; sessions go career → about you →
the role with a bridge between parts; Feedback is one click away from any step
and lists past sessions.
