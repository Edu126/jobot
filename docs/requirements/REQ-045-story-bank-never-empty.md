# REQ-045: The Story Bank should never feel empty

Date: 2026-09-27 (backfilled 2026-10-01)
Source: Eduardo
Status: Shipped (commit 6619351)

## What they asked for
An empty Story Bank should already suggest stories from the résumé.

## What they actually need
STAR stories are the raw material for the whole prep flow (mapping, answer cards, practice). A blank page stalls new users at the hardest step: remembering what they did.

## How we'll know it worked
A new user sees STAR drafts drawn from their own résumé and saves at least one without writing from scratch.
