# REQ-049: A real interview's question mix (and coach reactions that fit)

Date: 2026-10-10
Source: Eduardo, -edu voice sessions 46–47 (notes spoken into session 47)
Status: Building

## What they asked for
"All the questions in general are related to the job itself, not behavioral
questions of the person — a time you had a hard time conveying a message,
competing priorities with somebody else, your weaknesses, where you have room
to grow… seed ideas, not what I'm looking to answer — the idea is a better
mix." Also: "the questions we usually ask are the same." And: the coach's
summaries aren't what he wants — "okay, yeah, sure, that makes sense… great
work… five different levels based on the answer"; a repeat request should get
"yeah, for sure"; a bare "could you elaborate on this specific impact?" is
misleading — which answer is it about?

## What they actually need
Practice that feels like the real thing, including the uncomfortable,
about-the-person questions interviewers ask on purpose, and that doesn't
replay the same set every session. Reactions should sound human and reflect
the answer, without turning into praise that contradicts Feedback.

## How we'll know it worked
A standard session includes one question about the person, two sessions in a
row don't ask the same set, and the coach's reactions vary with the answer.
