# REQ-051: The live aid reads like something you'd say

Date: 2026-10-12
Source: Eduardo, -edu session 52
Status: Building

## What they asked for
"We have to make the statement from the user more conversational, cause here
there's no smooth transitions, like first, then, after, also — connectors that
make the conversation feel human. This reads so bad." Approved target: "Sure.
Right now I'm… Before that, at… What draws me to this role is… So, in short…"

## What they actually need
A prepared answer you can glance at mid-interview and say out loud as is —
one natural flow, not glued-together notes — without the tool inventing
anything about their career.

## How we'll know it worked
In "With my answers" mode the aid reads as one spoken answer with connectors,
and every fact and number in it is one the candidate wrote.
