# ADR-080: Prepared answers live in an account-level Answer Bank

Date: 2026-10-12
Status: Accepted
Relates to: REQ-052, ADR-050 (Story Bank keyed on the candidate), ADR-057

## Decision
Answers are built once, at account level, in an Answer Bank next to the
Story Bank: the candidate answers in their own words, the evaluator returns
gaps, they add real data, the answer is rewritten and re-evaluated. Per
interview, Get Ready only picks which bank answers to use (step 0) and adapts
them to the role; Practice validates them out loud.

## Why
Most interview questions repeat across companies (about you, conflict, a
mistake, how you work with data). Building answers inside Get Ready means
redoing the work for every interview; a bank keeps the effort and improves it
over time. Eduardo agreed 2026-10-12.
