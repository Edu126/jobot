# Founder roadmap — from vibe coder to company

Last updated: 2026-08-27

`vision.md` = where we're going. `milestones.md` = how the *product*
gets there. This = how **you, the founder**, walk there — starting from
where you actually are: vibe-coding, testing with friends, not (yet) a
business/solutions architect.

## The truth that removes the weight

You don't need to be a business architect. Companies aren't built from a
master plan — they're built from tight loops of *make something a few
people love → learn → expand*. Building + talking to users (what you
already do) **is** the hard part. Incorporation, fundraising, pricing,
team are **premature** until there's signal.

The dangerous myth is that there's a "set up the company" phase you're
skipping. There isn't. Right now your only job is to find out **if anyone
comes back.** Founders learn business *by building, just-in-time* — not
before. When something genuinely exceeds you, the answer isn't an MBA —
it's a co-founder/advisor for *that* specific piece.

## The path, from your side

| Where you are | Your ONE job | Company / money |
|---|---|---|
| **Today** — vibe code, friends, no analytics | Prove *someone* gets value repeatedly | Nothing. Your time. |
| **Signal** (Phase 0–1) | Instrument + get ~10 users who are **not** friends, by hand | Nothing yet. "Do things that don't scale." |
| **Traction** (Phase 1–2) | A small group loves it and returns | **Charge money** (the ultimate validation) · maybe incorporate · maybe a small pre-seed *if* you want to go faster |
| **Scale** (Phase 3–4) | Partnerships, team | Business architecture matters here — by then you'll have learned it or brought someone who knows |

## Next ~90 days (nothing here needs business expertise)

1. **Instrument** — stop flying blind (Phase 0).
2. **10 strangers** — go from friends to strangers recruited by hand. The
   most revealing and scariest jump.
3. **Do they come back?** — look at the number. Yes → you have a company.
   No → fix the product before anything else.

No incorporation, no pitch deck, no investor yet. Those come *after* the
retention number gives permission.

## The advisory system we'll build (later — not now)

Idea to document as a **skill**: a founder advisory panel with distinct
roles that we can invoke at the right stage —
- **God-mode advisor** — zoom-out, blind spots, what we're over/under-carrying
- **Advisor** — grounded next-step business guidance, just-in-time
- **Technical partner / lead** — architecture & build sequencing
- **Developer** — execution

Before building it, a **founder-led** exploration (Eduardo drives, agents
don't just dump): *do similar founder-agent systems already exist, and how
have they been integrated at each stage?* — captured in
`docs/research/RESEARCH-PLAN-market-thesis.md` (pillar 4). Goal is to learn
the shape ourselves, not absorb someone else's AI-construction bias.
