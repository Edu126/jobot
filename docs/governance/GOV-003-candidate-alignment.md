# GOV-003: We are paid by the candidate, never against them

Date: 2026-08-27
Status: Accepted
Related: product `vision.md`, architecture `vision.md` non-goal
("no recruiter/employer surface"), GOV-001, GOV-002

## The rule

jobot serves the job seeker. The moment we take money from an employer or
recruiter to **influence what the candidate sees** (which jobs, which
ranking, which advice), we betray the user and become the LinkedIn
business model jobot exists to sidestep.

Permitted revenue is **candidate-aligned**:
- Paid by the candidate (freemium, subscription, agent/volume tiers).
- Paid by partners who **benefit the candidate** (education platforms,
  coaching) — surfaced only when it genuinely closes the user's real gap,
  never as disguised advertising that distorts ranking or advice.

## Forbidden

- Employer/recruiter pay-to-rank, pay-to-surface, or pay-to-access-the-candidate.
- Any incentive that makes jobot's advice serve someone other than the
  user in front of it.

## Why it's governance, not just strategy

This is a data/trust boundary. The candidate hands jobot their most
sensitive career data on the premise that jobot is *their* agent. Any
revenue that inverts that alignment is an abuse of that data trust, even
if technically consensual. When a future revenue idea touches employers,
it must be checked against this note first.
